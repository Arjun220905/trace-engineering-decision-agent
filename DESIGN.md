# Trace

Trace follows an engineering meeting from transcript through review to delivery. The meeting list puts import, search, and review status first. The import dialog places optional settings and direct IDs behind disclosure controls.

- Canvas: white and soft slate, with steel blue for controls and links, green for completed work, and red for destructive actions.
- Type: DM Sans for the interface and monospace for transcript timestamps.
- Layout: a compact sidebar, a meeting list with review state, and transcript evidence beside the draft. The workflow stacks on mobile.
- Workspace settings: distinct sections for defaults, members, access provisioning, and recent audit events.
